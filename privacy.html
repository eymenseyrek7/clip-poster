Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Eymen Clip Poster ("the app") is a personal tool operated by Eymen Seyrek ("the owner") for publishing the owner's own video clips to the owner's own social media accounts.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile information of the owner's own account (open ID, display name, avatar) to confirm which account is connected, and permission to upload or publish videos to that account.
  • Instagram: basic profile information and media insights (views, likes, comments) of the owner's own professional account, and permission to publish Reels to that account.

The app does not access, collect or store information about any other person.

How data is stored

Access tokens and post statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared with or transferred to any third party, and are not used for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from TikTok (Settings → Security → Apps and services) or Instagram (Settings → Apps and websites). Revoking access and deleting the local data folder removes all data the app holds.
